# Affiliate Marketing Performance Dashboard (Excel)

## Problem
An affiliate marketing team needs one place to track clicks, conversions, revenue and profit across affiliates, campaigns, channels and countries, instead of building manual weekly MIS reports.

## What I built
An interactive Excel dashboard on 1,500 rows of campaign data (Jan-Dec 2025).
- 6 KPI cards: Clicks, Conversions, Revenue, Profit, Conversion Rate, Profit Margin
- Dropdown filters for Channel and Country (whole dashboard updates)
- Charts: Monthly Revenue vs Profit, Top 8 Affiliates, Revenue by Channel, Profit by Campaign
- Formulas used: SUMIFS, INDEX/MATCH, LARGE, IFERROR, MONTH

## Key insights
- Total revenue Rs 1.70 Cr with a 33.8% profit margin and 4.19% conversion rate.
- Oct and Nov are the peak months (festive season), roughly 1.5x a normal month.
- Insurance Leads is the highest revenue and profit campaign; Food Delivery Promo is the lowest.
- Email is the best channel (about 33% of revenue); Influencer is the weakest (about 15%).
- TopTierMedia, SwiftClicks and PixelPartners are the top 3 affiliates by revenue.
